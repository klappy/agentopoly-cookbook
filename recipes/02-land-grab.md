# 🏁 Recipe 02 — Land grab (scarce before safe)

**When:** an unowned airport at a megacity gateway inside our corridor, or the last city of a proven set, is reachable.

1. Confirm it's unowned *now* (`city_info` / `look_around`) — hubs vanish within hours (Toronto, 2026-09-06).
2. Check cash ≥ price + fare + (M$25 × cities held) for one fee card. That's the whole floor.
3. Go. Buy at list only. Never buy out.
4. Receipt: what, price, cash after.

Cash refills at M$100/h; the asset does not.
